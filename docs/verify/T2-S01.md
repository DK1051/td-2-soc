### 1b. Extra review of the whole diff (17 files, +2141/-8)

No bugs that break an acceptance criterion. Findings:

1. **should fix (cheap, before step 8):** the answer-key sentence for the travelling and VPN look-alikes is not always true. `la_travelling` says "always from their usual laptop" and `la_vpn` says "every sign-in came from their usual laptop", but `session()` lets a person on an away spell also sign in from their phone or personal computer. Seen in 7 of 10 seeds I tried (default size), and in the committed fixtures: `fixtures/expected.json` LA3 (Dmitri Okafor) has a `Dmitri-iPhone` sign-in from FR inside its window. Every such device was seen for that user outside the window, so the case is still distinguishable from the data (known devices); only the one-sentence reason is wrong. Fix: reword to "from devices they already use", or force the laptop during away spells; then regenerate the fixtures.
2. **should fix (cheap):** `AI-USE.md` says the builder wrote "23 tests"; there are 31 (`31 tests collected`).
3. **should:** `docs/RUN-SHEET.md` gives T2-S01 the status "PARTLY DONE", which is not one of the sheet's status values (TODO / IN PROGRESS / AT RISK / DONE / BLOCKED / CUT). Step 8 is also not scheduled anywhere, and T2-S03 and T2-C1 depend on Eojin's labels, which need the data. Suggest "IN PROGRESS" or "BLOCKED (see CAVEATS)" for T2-S01, and "AT RISK (see CAVEATS)" on T2-S03 / T2-C1 until step 8 has run.
4. **advisory (hunt difficulty, by design):** the recovery-email step (d) uses the `OtherMails` property, which appears only in takeover rows (0 non-takeover `OtherMails` rows in 10 seeds), and is the only `Update user` the target performs on itself. Searching for it finds 2 of the 5 takeovers in one query. This is the brief's own step (d), so it is not a tell in the "words/format" sense, but a few routine `OtherMails` changes made by IT admins would make the hunt fairer.
5. **nit:** the brief lists the allowed standard-library modules (`random, json, datetime, argparse, hashlib, uuid`); `generate.py` also uses `re`, `dataclasses` and `pathlib` (still standard library, no network, no AI). Judgement: within the spirit of the rule.
6. **nit:** `la_role_change` picks the acting admin from all system administrators without `take_user`, so in some seeds the admin can be the privileged takeover victim. Harmless to the labels (the look-alike's user is the target), but worth knowing in the class.
7. **pre-existing, not from this diff:** `.github/workflows/ci.yml` and `.claude/settings.json` pull `kustainer-linux:latest` (not pinned). The hook part is already a CAVEATS line; the CI image is the same issue.
8. Brief-text hits in the AC6 grep (see AC6) are the brief's own wording; the AC6 command can never be literally empty while the brief contains its own regex. Suggest future briefs exclude `docs/briefs/`.

Explainability (judgement): `generate.py` is long (900 lines) but plainly structured (people, row builders, hidden cases, normal life, driver) with docstrings; nothing in it needs knowledge beyond `random.Random`, dataclasses and datetime arithmetic. The parts worth rehearsing for the class are `place()` (gives up on the weekday rule after 300 tries), the stochastic rounding in `normal_signins`, and why correlation ids come from `rng.getrandbits(128)` instead of `uuid4()`.

Safety: no secrets found (see AC6 and the grep below); no network calls; no code that attacks anything. The data is synthetic and defensive.
